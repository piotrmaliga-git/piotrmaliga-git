## About me

I am a Software Engineer with over four years of hands-on experience building responsive and high-performance web applications. I hold an Engineering degree in Computer Science from the University of the National Education Commission and I am currently completing my Master’s degree in Cybersecurity at the Cracow University of Technology. My work focuses on creating intuitive and accessible applications, with strong attention to test coverage and clean architecture. I continuously develop my knowledge expertise while also expanding my knowledge across other areas, aiming to grow as a T-shaped developer. At the same time, I explore AI to design smarter and more efficient solutions. Right now, I am working on my graduate work on a comparative analysis of the effectiveness of language models in detecting phishing messages. As part of this research, I built a full-stack application - Phishing Email Analyzer. It allows users to analyze emails and receive detection results based on different models.
